MENTAL HEALTH WARNING SIGNS AND CRISIS INDICATORS

This document outlines critical warning signs that require immediate professional attention, crisis indicators, and emergency intervention guidelines.

================================================================================
SUICIDE RISK WARNING SIGNS
================================================================================

IMMEDIATE DANGER SIGNS (CALL 911 OR GO TO ER):
- Talking about wanting to die or kill oneself
- Looking for ways to kill oneself (searching online, obtaining means)
- Talking about feeling hopeless or having no reason to live
- Talking about being a burden to others
- Increasing use of alcohol or drugs
- Acting anxious or agitated
- Withdrawing from family and friends
- Changing eating and sleeping habits
- Showing rage or talking about seeking revenge
- Taking risks that could lead to death (reckless driving, unsafe sex)
- Giving away prized possessions
- Saying goodbye to loved ones
- Putting affairs in order, making a will
- Sudden improvement in mood after severe depression (may indicate decision made)

HIGH-RISK FACTORS:
- Previous suicide attempt
- Family history of suicide
- Access to lethal means (firearms, medications)
- Recent significant loss (relationship, job, loved one)
- Chronic pain or serious illness
- Substance abuse
- Mental health disorder (especially depression, bipolar, schizophrenia)
- Recent discharge from psychiatric hospital
- Social isolation, lack of support
- History of trauma or abuse
- LGBTQ+ youth without family support

VERBAL WARNING SIGNS:
- "I wish I were dead"
- "I can't go on anymore"
- "Everyone would be better off without me"
- "There's no point in living"
- "I just want the pain to stop"
- "I have no reason to stay alive"
- "I'm a burden to everyone"
- "You won't have to worry about me much longer"

BEHAVIORAL WARNING SIGNS:
- Researching suicide methods
- Stockpiling pills
- Obtaining weapons
- Writing suicide note
- Visiting or calling people to say goodbye
- Giving away belongings
- Sudden calmness after period of depression
- Increased substance use
- Reckless behavior
- Withdrawing from activities and people

================================================================================
SELF-HARM WARNING SIGNS
================================================================================

INDICATORS OF SELF-HARM:
- Unexplained cuts, burns, or bruises (often on arms, legs, stomach)
- Wearing long sleeves/pants even in warm weather (to hide injuries)
- Frequent "accidents" or injuries
- Possession of sharp objects (razors, knives, broken glass)
- Isolation and secretive behavior
- Difficulty managing emotions
- Statements about worthlessness or self-hatred
- Low self-esteem
- Relationship problems

COMMON SELF-HARM BEHAVIORS:
- Cutting or scratching skin
- Burning skin
- Hitting or punching self or objects
- Head banging
- Pulling hair
- Picking at wounds to prevent healing
- Embedding objects under skin
- Excessive scratching

RISK FACTORS FOR SELF-HARM:
- History of trauma or abuse
- Mental health conditions (depression, anxiety, BPD, PTSD)
- Difficulty expressing emotions
- Peer influence or social contagion
- Perfectionism
- Poor coping skills
- Substance abuse
- Bullying or social rejection

ESCALATION SIGNS:
- Increasing frequency of self-harm
- More severe injuries
- Less effort to hide injuries
- Self-harm in more visible locations
- Transition from self-harm to suicidal thoughts

================================================================================
PSYCHOTIC SYMPTOMS
================================================================================

HALLUCINATIONS:
- Hearing voices that others don't hear
- Seeing things that aren't there
- Feeling sensations on skin without cause
- Smelling odors others don't smell
- Tasting things that aren't present

DELUSIONS:
- Fixed false beliefs despite evidence to contrary
- Paranoia (belief others are plotting against you)
- Grandiosity (belief in special powers or importance)
- Belief that thoughts are being controlled
- Belief that thoughts are being broadcast
- Belief that external forces are controlling actions

DISORGANIZED THINKING:
- Incoherent or nonsensical speech
- Jumping between unrelated topics
- Making up words (neologisms)
- Inability to organize thoughts
- Difficulty following conversation

DISORGANIZED BEHAVIOR:
- Inappropriate or bizarre behavior
- Unpredictable agitation
- Childlike silliness
- Difficulty with daily tasks
- Catatonia (lack of movement or response)

NEGATIVE SYMPTOMS:
- Flat affect (no emotional expression)
- Lack of motivation
- Social withdrawal
- Inability to experience pleasure
- Poverty of speech

WHEN TO SEEK IMMEDIATE HELP:
- First episode of psychotic symptoms
- Command hallucinations (voices telling you to harm self or others)
- Inability to distinguish reality from delusion
- Dangerous behavior due to psychotic symptoms
- Severe agitation or aggression
- Inability to care for self

================================================================================
SEVERE DEPRESSION INDICATORS
================================================================================

CRITICAL DEPRESSION SIGNS:
- Complete inability to get out of bed
- Cannot perform basic self-care (bathing, eating, hygiene)
- Total loss of interest in everything
- Severe psychomotor retardation (extreme slowing)
- Inability to work or function in any capacity
- Severe weight loss or gain (10%+ body weight)
- Persistent thoughts of death (not just suicidal ideation)
- Psychotic features (delusions, hallucinations)
- Catatonic features

TREATMENT-RESISTANT DEPRESSION:
- No improvement after 2+ antidepressant trials
- No response to therapy
- Worsening despite treatment
- Chronic, persistent symptoms for years
- Significant functional impairment despite treatment

POSTPARTUM DEPRESSION RED FLAGS:
- Thoughts of harming baby
- Inability to bond with baby
- Severe anxiety or panic attacks
- Thoughts of harming self
- Psychotic symptoms
- Inability to care for baby

================================================================================
SEVERE ANXIETY AND PANIC
================================================================================

PANIC DISORDER ESCALATION:
- Multiple panic attacks per day
- Constant fear of next panic attack
- Severe agoraphobia (unable to leave home)
- Complete avoidance of situations
- Inability to work or function
- Panic attacks lasting hours
- Physical symptoms mimicking heart attack

SEVERE GENERALIZED ANXIETY:
- Constant, uncontrollable worry
- Severe physical symptoms (chest pain, dizziness, nausea)
- Inability to sleep for days
- Cannot concentrate on anything
- Severe muscle tension and pain
- Inability to function in daily life

OBSESSIVE-COMPULSIVE DISORDER SEVERITY:
- Compulsions taking hours per day (4+ hours)
- Unable to resist compulsions
- Severe distress when unable to perform rituals
- Compulsions causing physical harm (excessive washing causing skin damage)
- Complete interference with daily functioning
- Contamination fears preventing eating or leaving home

================================================================================
MANIC EPISODE WARNING SIGNS
================================================================================

MANIC SYMPTOMS (BIPOLAR DISORDER):
- Extremely elevated or irritable mood
- Decreased need for sleep (feels rested after 2-3 hours)
- Grandiosity or inflated self-esteem
- More talkative than usual, pressured speech
- Racing thoughts, flight of ideas
- Easily distracted
- Increased goal-directed activity
- Excessive involvement in risky activities (spending sprees, sexual indiscretions, foolish investments)

DANGEROUS MANIC BEHAVIORS:
- Reckless driving
- Unsafe sexual behavior
- Spending entire savings
- Quitting job impulsively
- Giving away possessions
- Starting multiple unrealistic projects
- Aggressive or hostile behavior
- Psychotic features during mania

MIXED EPISODES (MOST DANGEROUS):
- Symptoms of both mania and depression simultaneously
- High energy with depressed mood
- Agitation with suicidal thoughts
- Highest risk for suicide
- Severe irritability and aggression

================================================================================
EATING DISORDER MEDICAL EMERGENCIES
================================================================================

ANOREXIA NERVOSA RED FLAGS:
- Severe weight loss (BMI < 15)
- Fainting or dizziness
- Irregular heartbeat
- Low blood pressure
- Electrolyte imbalances
- Amenorrhea (loss of period)
- Lanugo (fine body hair)
- Brittle hair and nails
- Refusal to eat despite medical danger

BULIMIA NERVOSA RED FLAGS:
- Frequent purging (multiple times per day)
- Electrolyte imbalances
- Swollen salivary glands
- Tooth decay
- Esophageal damage
- Irregular heartbeat
- Dehydration

BINGE EATING DISORDER RED FLAGS:
- Rapid weight gain
- Eating to point of physical pain
- Eating in secret
- Severe distress about eating
- Medical complications (diabetes, heart disease)

================================================================================
SUBSTANCE USE CRISIS INDICATORS
================================================================================

OVERDOSE SIGNS (CALL 911):
- Unconsciousness or difficulty staying conscious
- Slow or stopped breathing
- Choking or gurgling sounds
- Blue lips or fingernails
- Slow or erratic pulse
- Confusion or delirium
- Seizures
- Extreme agitation or paranoia

WITHDRAWAL EMERGENCIES:
- Severe tremors or shaking
- Hallucinations
- Seizures
- Severe confusion
- Rapid heartbeat
- High fever
- Severe nausea and vomiting

DANGEROUS SUBSTANCE USE PATTERNS:
- Using alone
- Increasing doses
- Mixing substances (especially alcohol + opioids)
- Using despite medical problems
- Inability to stop despite wanting to
- Withdrawal symptoms when not using

================================================================================
TRAUMA AND PTSD CRISIS SIGNS
================================================================================

ACUTE STRESS REACTION:
- Severe dissociation (feeling detached from reality)
- Flashbacks that feel completely real
- Inability to distinguish past from present
- Severe panic during flashbacks
- Self-harm during dissociative states
- Dangerous behavior during flashbacks

COMPLEX PTSD ESCALATION:
- Severe emotional dysregulation
- Intense anger or rage
- Self-destructive behavior
- Relationship crises
- Suicidal ideation
- Severe dissociation

================================================================================
PERSONALITY DISORDER CRISIS PATTERNS
================================================================================

BORDERLINE PERSONALITY DISORDER:
- Intense fear of abandonment leading to crisis
- Severe mood swings (hours, not days)
- Impulsive self-destructive behavior
- Self-harm or suicide attempts
- Intense, unstable relationships in crisis
- Severe dissociation or paranoia under stress

SEVERE PERSONALITY DISORDER FEATURES:
- Complete inability to maintain relationships
- Chronic suicidal behavior
- Severe self-harm
- Inability to work or function
- Legal problems
- Homelessness or housing instability

================================================================================
CHILD AND ADOLESCENT WARNING SIGNS
================================================================================

CHILDREN (UNDER 12):
- Talk of wanting to die
- Extreme behavior changes
- Withdrawal from friends and activities
- Giving away favorite toys
- Running away or talking about running away
- Severe decline in school performance
- Aggressive behavior
- Frequent physical complaints (headaches, stomachaches)

ADOLESCENTS (12-18):
- All adult warning signs apply
- Increased risk-taking behavior
- Substance experimentation
- Social media posts about death or suicide
- Cyberbullying (victim or perpetrator)
- Sexual risk-taking
- Truancy or school refusal
- Conflict with parents escalating

================================================================================
OLDER ADULT WARNING SIGNS
================================================================================

UNIQUE RISKS FOR ELDERLY:
- Social isolation after loss of spouse
- Chronic pain or terminal illness
- Loss of independence
- Financial stress
- Hoarding medications
- Refusing medical care
- Giving away possessions
- Updating will or funeral arrangements
- Talking about being a burden

DEMENTIA-RELATED CONCERNS:
- Severe confusion or disorientation
- Wandering and getting lost
- Aggressive behavior
- Severe paranoia
- Inability to recognize loved ones
- Refusal to eat or drink
- Severe agitation

================================================================================
IMMEDIATE INTERVENTION REQUIRED
================================================================================

CALL 911 OR GO TO EMERGENCY ROOM IF:
- Active suicidal plan with intent
- Suicide attempt in progress
- Severe self-harm requiring medical attention
- Psychotic symptoms with dangerous behavior
- Overdose or substance emergency
- Severe manic episode with reckless behavior
- Unable to care for basic needs
- Danger to others
- Medical emergency related to mental health (eating disorder complications, severe withdrawal)

CALL 988 SUICIDE & CRISIS LIFELINE IF:
- Suicidal thoughts without immediate plan
- Severe emotional distress
- Need crisis counseling
- Concerned about someone else
- Need resources and referrals

GO TO EMERGENCY ROOM IF:
- Suicidal thoughts and cannot keep self safe
- Severe symptoms requiring immediate psychiatric evaluation
- Need crisis stabilization
- Medication emergency
- No other mental health resources available

CRISIS TEXT LINE (Text HOME to 741741):
- Prefer text communication
- In crisis but not immediate danger
- Need someone to talk to
- Anxiety or panic attack
- Self-harm urges

================================================================================
SUPPORTING SOMEONE IN CRISIS
================================================================================

DO:
- Take all threats seriously
- Listen without judgment
- Ask directly: "Are you thinking about suicide?"
- Stay with person if possible
- Remove access to lethal means
- Call for professional help
- Be calm and supportive
- Let them know you care
- Follow up after crisis

DON'T:
- Leave person alone if in immediate danger
- Argue or try to talk them out of feelings
- Act shocked or judgmental
- Promise to keep suicide plan secret
- Minimize their pain
- Offer simplistic solutions
- Debate whether suicide is right or wrong

================================================================================
AFTER CRISIS INTERVENTION
================================================================================

FOLLOW-UP CARE:
- Ensure connection to mental health professional
- Schedule follow-up appointment before leaving ER
- Create safety plan
- Identify support system
- Remove or secure lethal means
- Monitor closely
- Continue treatment
- Address underlying issues

SAFETY PLANNING:
- Warning signs to watch for
- Coping strategies to use
- People to contact for support
- Professional resources
- Ways to make environment safe
- Reasons for living

================================================================================
CRISIS RESOURCES
================================================================================

NATIONAL RESOURCES:
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
- National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 1-800-273-8255
- SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357
- Veterans Crisis Line: 1-800-273-8255, press 1
- Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ youth): 1-866-488-7386
- Trans Lifeline: 1-877-565-8860
- National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233
- National Sexual Assault Hotline: 1-800-656-4673
- National Child Abuse Hotline: 1-800-4-A-CHILD

EMERGENCY:
- 911 for immediate danger
- Local emergency room
- Mobile crisis team (check local resources)
- Police welfare check (if cannot reach person)

================================================================================
IMPORTANT REMINDERS
================================================================================

- When in doubt, err on side of caution - seek help
- Crisis situations can change rapidly
- Professional assessment is always recommended
- You cannot "make" someone suicidal by asking
- Taking action can save a life
- Crisis is temporary, suicide is permanent
- Help is available 24/7
- Recovery is possible

This document is for educational purposes to help identify crisis situations requiring immediate intervention. It does not replace professional mental health assessment or emergency services.
